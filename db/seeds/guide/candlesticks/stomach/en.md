## How it looks

- Above the stomach: a long bearish bar followed by a bullish bar that opens and closes at or above the midpoint of the first body. Textbooks read it as a bullish reversal within a downtrend.
- Below the stomach: a long bullish bar followed by a bearish bar that opens and closes at or below the midpoint of the first body. Textbooks read it as a bearish reversal within an uptrend.

## What it tells you

The second bar traded from the start at a level that had already recovered half of the first body. It is notable because it goes against the first bar's move from the open.

## How Siglens detects it

The first bar's body must be at least 60% of its high-to-low range. The second bar must be the opposite color and open beyond the midpoint of the first body (at or above the midpoint for above the stomach, at or below for below the stomach). The preceding trend is not checked. Bulkowski's figures were measured on patterns that appeared after a trend (above the stomach after a decline, below the stomach after an advance), so they can't be applied without checking the trend first. A shape with no trend before it is just a two-bar wobble in a sideways market.

Thomas Bulkowski's data shows:

- Above the stomach: 66% bullish reversal, ranking 31st of 103 for overall performance. In another of his studies it ranked 1st among common bullish reversal candles, with a 10-day average gain of 2.74%.
- Below the stomach: 60% bearish reversal, ranking 59th of 103, in the middle.

The bullish version clearly did better than the bearish one. Above the stomach worked best where a pullback inside an uptrend ended, or near the yearly low (the lower third of the year's price range). Below the stomach worked best in a pullback rally inside a downtrend.

## Watch out for

Confirmation is a close beyond the far end of the pattern: above the high for above the stomach, below the low for below the stomach. Until then it is only a clue. It matters more near support (above) or resistance (below) with high volume on the second bar. A pattern in a sideways market ([ADX](/guide/indicators/adx) below 20) is weak, and below the stomach is on the weak side even on its own.
