## How it looks

Three big-bodied bars in the same direction appear in a row. Each bar opens inside the previous bar's body and closes beyond the previous close.

- Three white soldiers: three bullish bars, each closing higher. Textbooks read it as a bullish reversal after a decline.
- Three black crows: three bearish bars, each closing lower. Textbooks read it as a bearish reversal after an advance.

## What it tells you

One side won three times in a row, so it is read as a clue that the earlier trend has ended and strength is building in the opposite direction.

Thomas Bulkowski tallied what actually happened after patterns across decades of US stock charts and published the results in books. In his data the reversal rates (the share that actually turned in the expected direction) were high for both: 82% for three white soldiers and 78% for three black crows. Three white soldiers ranked 3rd of 103 candle patterns by reversal rate.

The two split, though, in his performance ranking, which orders patterns by how far price went afterward. Three black crows ranked 3rd, but three white soldiers didn't travel far after the breakout and placed only 32nd. They got the direction right but the move was small.

Part of the high reversal rate comes from the shape. Bulkowski counted a breakout in a direction when a later close moved above the pattern's highest high (below its lowest low for black crows). This pattern already ends near the high (near the low for black crows), so even a small further move easily counts as a breakout.

## How Siglens detects it

Siglens looks for three big same-color bars, each opening inside the previous body, that step in one direction like a staircase.

- All three bars are the same color.
- Each body is at least 60% of its own high-to-low range.
- The second and third bars open inside the previous bar's body. For white soldiers, each open is above the previous open and below the previous close.
- The preceding trend is not checked.

It carries more weight when:

- A clear trend in the opposite direction came before
- The bodies are large and the wicks are short
- Volume grows across the three bars
- It breaks through the 20-day [EMA](/guide/indicators/ema) or a key price level
- For black crows, the bars are large (Bulkowski's data showed better performance)

## Watch out for

If it appears after a big move in the same direction, it can signal that the move is running out of strength. If the third bar is unusually long, treat it as overbought (risen so far it may pull back; for black crows, oversold) and be wary of chasing. Bulkowski said three white soldiers appearing in a brief bounce inside a downtrend often saw the decline continue afterward. Near a major resistance or support, keep a failed breakout in mind.
